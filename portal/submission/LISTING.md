## itch.io Submission

### Basic Information

**Title:** Shadow Striker: Steel City

**Tagline:**  
One night. Three missions. Save Steel City.

**Short Description:**  
Tactical action combining flight combat, ground driving, and rooftop stealth. Stop a hijacked defence network and deliver the override before the city freezes. Free browser game with controller support.

**Full Description:**

Shadow Striker is a single-night tactical campaign where you defend Steel City from a hijacked defence network. Armed aircraft are targeting the heating grid, and you have until midnight to stop them.

**Gameplay:**

The game combines three distinct modes in a seamless story:

- **Flight Combat:** Pilot the Shadow Striker interceptor through aerial dogfights. Disable enemy command relays and evade pursuit drones across neon-lit city districts.
- **Tactical Driving:** Switch to the Nightblade vehicle for ground operations. Navigate checkpoints and deliver the heating override to the cathedral shelter.
- **Stealth Infiltration:** Grapple across rooftops at Kessler Cold Storage. Disable uplink dishes and extract the flight log without being detected.

Complete the main objectives in under 45 minutes, or take on optional challenges for additional story details and time attack modes.

**Features:**

- Story-driven single-player campaign
- Three gameplay modes with distinct controls and objectives
- Full controller support (Xbox, PlayStation, generic gamepad)
- Mobile-friendly touch controls
- No installs—runs directly in browser via WebGL
- Optional time challenges and score tracking

**Controls:**

**Keyboard/Mouse:**
- WASD: Movement / flight
- Mouse: Look / aim
- Space: Fire
- Shift: Boost
- E/Q: Barrel rolls (flight)
- R: Grapple (stealth)

**Gamepad:**
- Left stick: Move / fly

- Bumpers: Barrel rolls
- Face buttons: Context actions

**Mobile:**
- Touch joystick: Movement

**Technical Requirements:**

- Browser with WebGL 2.0 support (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari)
