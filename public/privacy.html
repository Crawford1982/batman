Privacy & cookies

Gotham After Dark is an unofficial fan-made browser game. You do not need an account to play.

Optional analytics

When analytics is configured, we ask before loading Google Analytics. If you allow it, Google receives basic visit and technical information and game events such as chapter starts, completions, elapsed play time and scores. This helps us understand traffic sources, device use and where the game needs improvement. We do not send player names, email addresses or account IDs. Google receives technical network information when requests are made to its service.

Advertising signals and ad personalisation are disabled. Declining analytics does not affect gameplay. Your choice is saved on this device for up to 180 days. Analytics cookies are configured for a maximum of 180 days and may be refreshed by later visits.

Choosing “No thanks” stops future game analytics and clears this site's accessible Google Analytics cookies. It does not delete information already collected. Browser blockers may also prevent analytics.

Other browser storage and hosting

The game may store local progress on your device. Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages; it processes network requests to deliver the site. Fonts and other externally hosted resources may also require requests to their providers. These requests are separate from optional analytics.

Read Google's privacy policy and GitHub's privacy statement for their handling of service data.

Contact

For questions, contact the project maintainer through the game's GitHub repository. Please do not post private information in public issues.
